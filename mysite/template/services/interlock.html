Overview

Hardscaping that survives freeze-thaw.

Most interlock failures — sinking, shifting, weeds pushing through — trace back to a poorly prepared base, not the pavers themselves. We excavate and compact properly before a single paver goes down, so your driveway or patio stays level for years, not one season.
