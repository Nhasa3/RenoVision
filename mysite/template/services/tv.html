Overview

A wall that anchors the room.

A feature wall is one of the highest-impact changes you can make to a living room for the cost. We design around your TV size, seating layout, and material preference — wood slats, stone veneer, electric fireplace, floating shelving — with every cable and cord run inside the wall.
